# Theoretical Alignment Dynamics Framework

Independent research project exploring dynamical alignment, stability analysis,
error propagation, and emergent geometry in high-dimensional adaptive systems.

## Components

- **ARS-core (Adaptive Relational Substrate):**
  Nonlinear dynamical system modeling convergence, divergence, and drift of internal states.

- **HERE-core:**
  Continuous geometric layer tracking emergent curvature, metric deformation, and structural stability.

- **EBA-core:**
  Coupling mechanism evaluating how local perturbations propagate globally.

## Research Focus

- Alignment drift as a dynamical phenomenon  
- Robustness under adversarial or stochastic perturbations  
- Stability boundaries and bifurcation behavior  
- Propagation and amplification of internal errors  
- Emergence of coherent or misaligned internal representations  
- Computationally efficient emergent-geometry modeling compared to GR-based frameworks
